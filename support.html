A little help.

Questions about Youcabulary, your learning data, or something that isn’t working? Get in touch.

Contact

developer@spudspicer.com

Please include your device, operating system, app version and what you were trying to do. Screenshots can help, but remove personal information first. Never send API keys or passwords.

Downloads and availability

Youcabulary is currently in development. Public App Store and other download links will be added to the home page when available.

Your learning data

The current app stores core learning data locally. Deleting a deck is not the same as deleting your learning history. For help with exports or removing personal data, contact us and describe which version and optional services you use.

Audio is unavailable

Study audio depends on a suitable voice for your learning language being available on your device. Check your device’s speech settings and the guidance shown inside Youcabulary.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy draft for an overview of local data and optional connected features.
